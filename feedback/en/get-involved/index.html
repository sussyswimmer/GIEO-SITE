What we're building

These are things GIEO is building, not results it has already completed.

  • Experiential trips to visit artisans and their children
  • Scholarship programs
  • Hygiene education
  • Donations of clothes and personal care products for girls and women
  • Career programs for high school, university and vocational training
  • Financial literacy
  • English workshops
  • A buddy system to help children prepare for graduation exams
