About GIEO

Girls' Initiative for Education and Opportunities

Girls' Initiative for Education and Opportunities (GIEO) is a student-led social initiative founded and operated by a group of high school students in Hanoi.

gieo

v./zɛw/

to sow

In Vietnamese, the word "gieo" means to sow, symbolizing the planting of seeds of hope, opportunity, and lasting change for girls growing up in rural and mountainous communities across Vietnam.

Our initiative combines cultural preservation with community development by creating modern lifestyle products that appeal to younger generations while celebrating the traditional brocade weaving of the H'mong people.

Supported by the IB Global Youth Action Fund

From the first ideas we put together to the people we met along the way, this season has been full of learning, creating, and growing together.

Three pillars

  • Economic Empowerment

    We create sustainable income opportunities for H'mong women artisans by designing and marketing modern products that incorporate traditional brocade craftsmanship. By connecting their work with contemporary markets, we help preserve traditional weaving while strengthening financial independence for artisan families.

  • Education

    We invest in the education and personal development of artisans' children, particularly girls. Through scholarships, educational support, hygiene products, awareness programs, and life-skills education, we aim to remove barriers that prevent girls from pursuing their education and future ambitions.

  • Mentorship Network

    We are building a long-term mentorship ecosystem where older students, university graduates, and professionals return to inspire and support the next generation. By connecting girls with mentors from diverse backgrounds, we hope to broaden their perspectives, provide career guidance, and foster a cycle of community leadership in which each generation uplifts the next.

Vision

We envision thriving ethnic minority communities where traditional cultural heritage and modern opportunities grow together. Through sustainable economic development, women's empowerment, quality education, and strong community networks, we aspire to create an environment where every H'mong woman and girl has the resources, confidence, and freedom to shape her own future while preserving the cultural identity of her community for generations to come.

Mission

Our mission is to preserve the traditional brocade weaving heritage of the H'mong people by transforming it into products that resonate with modern consumers, creating sustainable livelihoods for women artisans and investing in the next generation. Through economic empowerment, educational opportunities, and mentorship, we seek to build a self-sustaining cycle of community development in which women gain financial independence, girls are empowered to pursue their aspirations, and future generations continue to strengthen and uplift their own communities.

Our partner

Chie Dù Pù Dù Pà

Our products are developed and produced in partnership with Chie Dù Pù Dù Pà, an enterprise established in 2011 with the mission of accompanying ethnic minority communities in preserving and developing traditional handicrafts, creating sustainable livelihoods, and spreading the beauty of local cultures.
